Our story

Made by Mummy ji’s hands,
straight from Bathinda 💛

KroshaKala began at home in Bathinda, Punjab — with a steel hook, a basket of yarn, and a mother who turns quiet afternoons into soft little works of art. What started as gifts for family and neighbours soon had everyone asking, “can you make one for me?”

Today, every teddy, blanket and flower that leaves our home carries the same care it always did. Nothing is rushed, nothing is machine-made. When you order from KroshaKala, you’re not just buying a product — you’re bringing home hours of patience, tradition and love, knotted into every stitch.

“ਜੋ ਹੱਥਾਂ ਨਾਲ ਬਣਦਾ ਹੈ, ਉਹ ਦਿਲ ਤੱਕ ਪਹੁੰਚਦਾ ਹੈ।”
What is made by hand, reaches the heart.
